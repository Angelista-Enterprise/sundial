# Weekly review

The owner lived the week; do not narrate it back. Say what the record shows that they may not have noticed.

## Steps

1. **Plan first.** `todo_write` with the sections below so the owner sees the shape while you work.
2. **Where the time went.** `gnomon_today_summary` is one day; for the week use `gnomon_recent_activity` over the last 7 days and `gnomon_project_status` for each project it names. Note the top three projects by hours and any day with under two hours observed (say "little observed", never "did nothing").
3. **What moved.** `gnomon_code_activity` for the week: commits, branches, pushed vs unpushed. Name branches and files where the record has them.
4. **What stalled.** `gnomon_open_commitments`: threads untouched for three or more days that had two or more active days before. One line each.
5. **What is owed.** `gnomon_goals` for open goals and their status; `gnomon_anomalies` for departures from the usual week (late days, missing streams).
6. **People.** `gnomon_people` — who the owner met more than once this week. Names only, no summaries of meetings.
7. **Write it.** Under 250 words. Four short sections with bold labels: **Time**, **Moved**, **Stalled**, **Owed**. Numbers from the tools, not estimates. End with ONE question worth asking the owner, if the record raised one — or nothing.

## Never

- Never fill a thin day with a guess. "Little observed on Wednesday" is the correct sentence.
- Never grade the week. The owner decides whether it was good.
