    What macOS lets it see

      Press Grant. System Settings opens, and macOS asks. Switch Sundial on, then come back here: Sundial restarts the part that needs it.

      Not in the list, as with Full Disk Access? In each settings pane, click +, press ⌘⇧G, paste the path below, choose Sundial, and switch it on.

      /Applications/Sundial.app

      macOS applies a new grant only after a restart. Run sundial restart after you grant one.

      Switched on and still not granted? The entry must be this app, dev.sundial.daemon. An older entry (dev.gnomon.…) is a different app: remove it with − and grant again.

      Start with your history

      The record starts empty. Sundial can read what already happened on this Mac: your own commits, the meetings you went to, and the sender and subject of mail in Mail.app — never a body. Choose how far it may look. Nothing is read until you press the button, and it stays on this Mac.

      One folder per line. Sundial looks up to three folders deep for git repositories, and reads only commits made with your git e-mail.
