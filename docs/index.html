# Sundial documentation Sundial is local-first software for macOS: it records what you work on into a private record on your Mac. Gnomon is the assistant inside it that reads the record and talks to you. ## Use it - [Getting started](/sundial/docs/getting-started/): install, sign in, the setup page, your history. - [Permissions](/sundial/docs/permissions/): what each macOS permission lets Sundial see, and how to grant or reset it. - [Models](/sundial/docs/models/): give Gnomon a model, local or hosted, and what leaves your Mac. - [Privacy](/sundial/docs/privacy/): what is recorded, what is cleaned, how long it is kept, how to delete it. - [Using Gnomon](/sundial/docs/using-gnomon/): the board, the chat, notices, approvals, and Claude Code. - [Troubleshooting](/sundial/docs/troubleshooting/): symptom, cause, fix. ## Understand it - [Why Sundial](/sundial/docs/why-sundial/): the rules the project holds itself to. - [Architecture](/sundial/docs/architecture/): how an observation travels from a sensor to the knowledge base. ## Work on it - [Development](/sundial/docs/development/): build, test, run a test install, and the conventions. - [Contributing](https://github.com/Angelista-Enterprise/sundial/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Security](https://github.com/Angelista-Enterprise/sundial/blob/develop/SECURITY.md).

No counting on these pages. Only the demo counts, and only what you do in it — a card placed, a question asked, which model answered — with GoatCounter: never the words you type, no cookies, no personal data. The only thing kept in your browser is a ten-minute cache of GitHub’s answers on the changelog and feedback pages, and your light-or-dark choice. Sundial itself, the app you install, sends nothing to us — see Privacy.
